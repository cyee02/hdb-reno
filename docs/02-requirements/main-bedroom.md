## Must have (both designs)
- **Queen bed** (1530 × 1910), flush in the **top-right corner**.
- **Wardrobe for 2**, with separate **His and Hers** space. Full height (2600), 600 D, top cabinets above 2050. Drawn **without doors (open)** so the inside shows.
  - Per person: a long-hang column (rod at 1850) + a column with 3 drawers and short hang (rod at 1750).
- **TV in front of the bed.**
- **Study / vanity table** + chair.
- **Shared cosmetics space** both of you can reach: counter at 900 over 3 drawers, mirror 950–1800, shelf at 1850 with an LED strip.

**West wall, from the window to the lobby:**
- **Vanity** (shared cosmetics): **600 long** × 500 D at the window end — **floating top** fixed to the walls on three sides, open below for the stool. **No drawers, no mirror.** Daylight from the window.
- **Dividing wall** (full height, 600 deep) between the vanity and the hanging area.
- **Hanging area — not carpentry:** wall-mounted **standards + brackets holding hanging poles** with a wire shelf on top (like the reference photo of a metal wardrobe system), **2040 long × 600 D**.
  - Upper pole full length at 1900; window end: **lower pole at 1000** for double-hang (shirts over trousers).
  - Middle: **stack of 4 pull-out wire baskets** (to ~870) under short hang — folded clothes / accessories.
  - Toilet end: long hang.
  - Wire shelf at 2050 for boxes / folded items.
  - Closed with **white bi-fold doors** (3 pairs, ~340 leaves, floor to ceiling) like the reference video; each pair folds out ~340 into the ~945 aisle.
- **Cupboard 2** (toilet end): **full height**, 650 × 550, **facing the lobby / WC**. Towels on shelves at the bottom (to 850), **open middle** (850–1600), boxes / seldom-used items on top (above 1600).
- **Full-length mirror** (600 × 1800) on the **lobby's south wall — on your right as you walk in** (clear of the door swing).

**Rest of the room:**
- **Bed:** queen, top-right, **headboard on the window wall**, 900 high (the sill). Get in from the left — ~945 mm aisle to the glass.
- **TV feature wall:** full-height (2600), 150 thick, **timber-slat** partition at the foot of the bed, 1625 long. **50" TV** on the bed side, centred, ~1.7 m from the pillows. Hides the TV cabling.
- **L-shaped study table, no cabinets below:** 1625 × 530 along the back of the feature wall + 1550 × 520 down the east wall. Supported by end panels and one corner post (plus fixing to the feature wall / east wall).
  - **Wall cabinets above the east-wall leg** (350 D, from 1450 to the ceiling, 4 doors) with an LED task light underneath.
  - **Monitor: PRISM+ X340 PRO** (34" 21:9, 1500R curved, ~810 × 361 mm without stand) on a **VESA 100 wall arm** (needs PRISM+'s custom spacers) on the back of the feature wall, centred on the first chair, screen centre ~1080 (bottom edge ~150 above the desk). The included stand isn't used. Cables run inside the feature wall with the TV's.
  - **2 office chairs fit:** one facing the feature wall, one facing the east wall (~650 footprint each, ~100 apart).
- **Hook rail** on the south wall between the end of the study table and the WC door: timber rail, 1100 long, 7 hooks at 1650 — for **towels, bags and clothes**.
