## Must have
- **Door:** glass sliding door that opens in the middle (two leaves), sliding on the **kitchen side**. Prefer a top-hung track with no floor rail so the robot vacuum can cross.
- **Washer + dryer (left side):** separate front-load units, **side by side** (not stacked, not a combo unit), built into carpentry. ~600 W × 600 D × 850 H each.
- **Folding counter:** worktop over the washer + dryer, wall to wall (1300 × 680 mm, top at ~920 mm), used to fold clothes.
- **iPad platform** on the folding counter, to watch shows while folding: **floating plank** on concealed wall brackets along the back wall, **full length (1300) × 160 D × 25 thick, top at 1000 mm from the floor** (~55 mm gap above the counter). Shallow so ~520 mm of counter depth stays free. iPad in the middle, **mini cactus** beside it.
  - The Steigen frame lowers over this area — don't lower it below ~1.2 m while the iPad is on the ledge.
  - Nice to have: a USB / power point near the platform for charging the iPad.
- **Tall cabinet (right side), 740 W × 600 D × 2300 H — arranged per owner's sketch.** Seen from the yard:
  - **Left column (416 W, next to the sink):**
    - Floor: open bay for the **robot vacuum + self-emptying dock** (650 H). Dock **plumbed in**: water inlet + floor drain behind it.
    - Shelf at 650: **detergent + softener bottles** and a **basket for reusable plastic bags** (Daiso-type).
    - Shelf at 1150: pegs bin + laundry mesh-bag bin (suggested).
  - **Right column (270 W, next to the kitchen wall):**
    - Open niche for the **Dyson stick vacuum** on its floor dock (to 1350 H), with a charging socket inside.
    - On top (shelf at 1350): **basket of laundry pods**.
  - **Hanger rod across the full width at 1900 mm** — for storing hangers only, within easy reach; laundry items sit below it.
  - **Top shelf at 1980**: overstock refills, spare sponges / gloves (suggested).
  - Doors over the whole cabinet except the robot bay (not drawn in renders so the inside is visible).
- **Utility sink (right side, beside the tall cabinet):** 560 W × 600 D unit with a stainless undermount basin **460 × 420 × 300 deep** — big enough to scrub a shoe — and a gooseneck tap.
- **Ceiling laundry rack: Steigen Solar Mini.** Body 1100 × 500 mm (installation clearance 1100 × 600), drying frame up to 1650 mm long (1800 mm clearance). Mounted over the walkway + folding counter, lengthwise along the yard, so it never lowers onto the tall cabinet.
- No grilles on the service yard / balcony openings (keep open, per HDB 3D render).

## Open questions
- **Tall cabinet covers the small window next to the door** (the 580 mm window at the south end of the kitchen/yard wall sits right behind the cabinet). Options: accept it (frosted glass / back panel), drop that window, or swap the tall cabinet and the sink (then the cabinet partly blocks the yard's outer opening instead).
- Washer and dryer bays are tight: 1300 mm interior leaves only ~25–50 mm spare around two 600 mm machines. Confirm machine widths (≤ 600 mm) and the real yard width before buying.
- Counter clearance over machines is ~40 mm (850 mm machines, counter underside at 890 mm). Check the machines' exact heights (some are 845–870 mm).
- Exact robot vacuum model (dock size varies; bay allows ~446 W × 650 H).
- Confirm with Steigen that the Solar Mini can be installed with 1720 mm length clearance (their diagram asks for up to 1800 mm), and the ceiling power point position.
- Dyson niche is 270 mm wide — check the floor dock / stand width of the exact Dyson model.
- Hanger rod height 1900 mm — adjust to your reach (the rod can go lower; the pods basket on the Dyson column must stay below the hangers).
