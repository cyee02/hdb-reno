## Must have
- **Whiteboard door:** the door's outer face (the side seen from the foyer / living room) becomes a **full-length white board**, ~740 W × 2020 H. Use a **magnetic whiteboard sheet or whiteboard film stuck on** — no screws, no drilling. Keep it off the rubber seal / frame and cut it around the handle so the door still closes and seals.
- **L-shaped, full-height rack** along the **west wall (long leg)** and the **south wall (short leg)**, per owner's sketch:
  - Long leg: **600 D** × full length (2560), split into 3 bays: 1280 (north, by the door) / 830 / 450 (corner).
  - Short leg: **450 D** × 750 along the south wall, to the east wall.
  - **Shelves only from waist level up: 950, 1330, 1710, 2090** + top shelf at **2470** (~130 mm below the ceiling). ~355 mm clear between shelves.
  - **Open below 950** (~925 mm clear) for **luggage and the kids' bike**. The 1280 bay by the door holds a 16" kids' bike lengthwise; luggage (up to a 28" case standing up, ~780 H) under the other bays.
  - Low tie bars at ~150 mm on the wall sides only — front left open so things roll in.
  - **Free-standing**: made-to-measure boltless steel rack, white powder coat. Bays bolted to each other, **not** to the walls.

## Nice to have
- Storage boxes / labelled tubs on the shelves (illustrated in the model).
- Magnetic marker tray / eraser holder on the whiteboard (magnetic, not screwed).


## Avoid / don't want
- Anything screwed, drilled or glued into the shelter walls, ceiling or door frame.
- Rack blocking the ventilation sleeves / covers on the walls — check where they are on site and leave them reachable.
