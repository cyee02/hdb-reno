## Appliance tower + removable oven (2026-11-01)
- **Full-height tower on the yard wall** (where the fridge was), **830 W × 600 D × 2600 H**:
  - 2 deep drawers at the bottom (100–700).
  - **Niche 1 (720–1260):** pull-out tray with the **countertop oven** (~480 W) + **air fryer** (~240 W).
  - **Niche 2 (1290–1760):** pull-out tray with the **microwave** (~500 W).
  - Cabinet doors from 1790 to the ceiling.
  - Trays slide out **380** on full-extension runners (in Blender: HDB Reno tab > "Kitchen … tray (pull out)", `scene["door_kitchen_tray_1/2"]`).
- **No built-in oven:** the oven, air fryer and microwave are ordinary countertop appliances, plugged into a **socket at the back of each niche** — lift out to replace or clean.
- **Use them pulled out.** Countertop ovens / air fryers vent hot air from the top and back (roughly 50–80 mm at the sides, ~100 at the back, 130+ above when used under a cabinet). Pulling the tray out before cooking puts the vents outside the niche, as in the reference. Also: heat-resistant lining or a stainless sheet on the niche ceiling, ventilation slots at the back, and runners rated for the weight (~25 kg).
- With a tray pulled out, the walkway between the island end and the tower drops from ~855 to ~480 — fine while cooking, push it back in afterwards.
- Under the hob: deep pot / pan drawers.
- Each niche needs its own switched socket; oven + air fryer together can draw ~3 kW, so put that niche on its own circuit.
